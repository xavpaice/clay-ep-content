# Welcome to Clay.nz

Clay.nz is an online pottery gallery and shop for New Zealand ceramic artists. This portal provides installation, configuration, and operational documentation for your deployment.

## Quick Start

1. Review the [Requirements](installation/requirements) for your environment
2. Follow the [Helm Installation](installation/helm) guide
3. Configure [Ingress and TLS](configuration/ingress) for your domain
4. Set up [Email](configuration/email) for order notifications

{{#if entitlements.enableFiringLogs}}
## Firing Logs

Your license includes the Firing Logs feature, which lets sellers track kiln sessions and temperature readings. See [Firing Logs Configuration](configuration/firing-logs) for setup details.
{{/if}}
